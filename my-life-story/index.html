but the most trouble i got in when i was in 3rd grade was the time i threw a bag of blue water out the bus window. then this stupid kid named jake dembo told the bus driver and the bus driver made me sit at the front of the bus for the rest of the year. the bus driver also told my parents and my teacher and for some reason they all got mad at me too. even though it wasnt even that bad because i made sure there were no cars behind us before i threw the bag out so it didnt hit anyone. on the second to last day of 3rd grade we had a party and i was so happy to finally eat yummy snacks since at my house we didnt have anything yummy so i ate the pretzels and then i also at the rest of the salt from the bag. then i threw up in the private bathroom and my dad came to pick me up even though it was the end of the day so i couldve took the bus home anyway.
