Seattle

birth

i was born at 5:28 AM on sunday, may 22, 2005, at swedish hospital in seattle, washington, usa, earth, the solar system, milky way, universe. my parents named me Lorelai Alden Harris. then, i almost died becuase i was sick but liuckily I survived. At some point after that, i broke my leg because my mom fell down the stairs when she was holding me. Also, i had a dog named Leena. I didn't have a brother yet. Also i lived at 4967 12th ave S Seattle, Washington, USA (earth, the solar system, milky way, universe). My leg recovered shortly after the incident (probably), although my brain may still be suffering (i never got tested). I had a mom and a dad and i still do. also i had grandparents and i also still have them. i lived at a house with some bedrooms and bathrooms and a kitchen and garage and living room and dining room and closets and hallways and stairs (the ones i broke my leg from). I changed my own diapers at some point.

i moved to 11719 se 58th st bellevue wa 98006 in august 2009 (i think). i still had a dog. and also a brother. my grandparents lived in seattle, so sometimes i visited them. my other grandparents lived in oregon.


Newport Heights

i began elementary school in september, 2010 at Newport Heights Elementary. My kindergarten teacher one time told me "thanks for taking off your jacket lorelai"when i was still wearing my jacket, so i thought she was stupid. one time she made me go to the principal's office during lunch because she is stupid. also sometiems i had chocolate milk for lunch and sometimes they played movies in the cafeteria. i remember it was really cold. thats why i was wearing a jacket that one time. one time i told my friend to go into the boys bathroom and then she got in trouble. my mom got in a car accident when i was in kindergarten or 1st grade because some lady ran a stop sign then our car was totaled so we got a new one. i still had a dog. in the summer i went to YMCA camp EVERY SINGLE DAY, and i rode my bike up a super steep hill every day to get there so i was in pretty good shape. but i was short.

in first grade, my teacher was nicer. it was still cold though. i read some books in the classroom next to mine becuase that teacher taight our class reading and my teacher taught her class science or something. there was a forest behinf the classroom. some kids got lice. also at some point we had to read with 5th graders. my PE teacher was named Mr. O (i think). there was an art teacher but i forgot her name. also there was a music teacher and his name was Mr falleto (i probably spelled that wrong). In first grade i took a test and passed,


Spiritridge

so then for 2nd grade i went to a different school named spiritridge elementary. it was far away from my house so i had to take a bus and my bus stop was at my first elementary school. my bus driver didnt like me but i forgot why. i went to before school care at my first elementary school and it was called dolphin club and it was so boring and stupid. i had to sit at the front of the bus but i dont remember why. one time in 2nd grade i went to the principals office but it was actually good because it was raining at recess and everyone wanted to go inside but they werent allowed. except for me and my friend who were at the principal's office. there was a library right in front of the office and then next to it were doors going to the playground so i could see everyone at recess while we were sitting there. my teacher was named Ms. Mintz. on fridays, you could buy popcorn for 25 cents but i never had money so i had to pick up pieces of trash instead. i accidentally squirted hand sanitizer into my friends eye. some kids in my class were really smart so they went to an even more advanced math class, but i was only kinda smart so i didnt do that. on the last day of 2nd grade my grandparents picked me up so i didnt have to take the bus home.

my 3rd grade teacher was named Mr. saltz and he was bald. i still went to spritridge for 3rd grade, but they ran oout of classrooms so we had to be in the preschool building. we werent supposed to play on the preschool playground but we did anyways becuase our teacher didnt care. we also had a private bathroom. one time i tied a kids showlaces together when we were sitting on the carpet and he watched me do it but then he still tripped over himself when he stood up. also one time i told him i bet he wears unicorn underwear and he said no so i said prove it and then he actually did and he was wearing american flag underwear. another time i was at recess and i had a sharpie and i accidentally drew on a kids jacket and he got mad and told the recess teacher and she sent me to the office but as i was walking to the office the fire alarm went off so i never actually got in trouble cause i just went to class after the fire alarm. also one time my teacher said if i didnt finish my times tables in time i would have to skip recess and then i didnt finish it but i went to recess anyway.

WHen i was in 3rd grade, my brother went to kindergarten at newport heights and since my bus stop was there i had to wait for him to be ready before i could leave becuase my mom would walk both of us. but he would take forever to dump all the sand out of shoes so i always was mad at him in the mornings because he would make himself late (i wasnt late because my bus came like 30 minutes after his school started but i was still mad because i had to wait). one morning i saw him put on his hoodie without a shirt under so then obviously i told my mom because i was mad at him cause he was so annoying and then she made him go put on a shirt. he got so mad he went to his room and pushed his dresser over and then one of the drawers broke because it was open when he pushed it.

the most trouble i got in when i was in 3rd grade was the time i threw a bag of blue water out the bus window. then this stupid kid named Jake Dembo told the bus driver and the bus driver made me sit at the front of the bus for the rest of the year. the bus driver also told my parents and my teacher and for some reason they all got mad at me too. even though it wasnt even that bad because i made sure there were no cars behind us before i threw the bag out so it didnt hit anyone. on the second to last day of 3rd grade we had a party and i was so happy to finally eat yummy snacks since at my house we didnt have anything yummy so i ate the pretzels and then i also at the rest of the salt from the bag. then i threw up in the private bathroom and my dad came to pick me up even though it was the end of the day so i couldve took the bus home anyway.


Somerset

After 3rd grade i never went to the principals office ever again because i turned into a good kid. In 4th grade the gifted program got moved to a school closer to my house because the other school was overflowing. It was called somerset elementary and the mascot is the superstars which is the dumbest mascot I have ever had. my teacher was named ms jackson and she yelled at us on the first day of school to make a point about first impressions and everyone in all the other classes thought she was scary because they could hear her yelling from their classroom but everyone in my class knew that she was actually nice (mostly). my elementary school was on a giant hill and it had a good view of the lake and buildings and stuff but my bus went on the side of the hill that didnt have a good view so i actaully didnt see it very often. also my bus stop was no longer at my first elementary school it was now at a random corner of the street. luckily since my bus stop was not the same as my brothers school anymore, i didnt have to wait for him to dump all the sand out of his shoes every morning anymore. my teacher was gay also. there was a tire swing on the playground and that was the best part of the school. during recess one time my friends all cornered me in the forest by the fence and they asked me if i liked a kid that went to my bus stop. i got mad at them because i thought they thought i liked him even though i definitely did not. also one time my friends got mad at me but i dont remember why but i remember thinking it was a dumb reason.

in 5th grade my teacher was british and i couldnt understand anything he said at first. i remember learning grammar in that class and it was the worst because i never knew the difference between a direct object and subject and the clauses and stuff. we grew milkweed bugs in the back of the classroom for science. my teacher said he rode his bike to school every day even though it was up a gigantic hill. on may 12th, 2011, my dog died and i was really sad so i cried at school the next day. then i wanted a new dog but my mom and my dad both said no. in 5th grade we had to do band instead of the normal music class where you just sing so i played the flute because it was the smallest instrument so it was the easiest to carry to school. unfortunately i was terrible at it because i was really slow at reading music and then even once i figured out what the notes said i still couldnt remember which holes to press for each note. my band teacher was nice though.
